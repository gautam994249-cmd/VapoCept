VapoCept  Ingredients

VapoCept combines six nutrients at measured amounts, each chosen for a specific role in supporting brain circulation, cellular energy, or antioxidant defense. Below is a breakdown of what each ingredient is and why it's included.

🍁

L-Arginine AKG 2:1 Extract

This is a bonded form of arginine paired with alpha-ketoglutarate, a compound that may help improve how the body absorbs and uses arginine over time. It's included to help support a steadier, longer-lasting effect throughout the day rather than a brief spike.
🍁

L-Arginine

L-Arginine is an amino acid the body can convert into nitric oxide, a molecule associated with relaxing and widening blood vessels. Wider, more relaxed blood vessels may allow more oxygen and nutrients to move through the bloodstream, including to the brain.
🍁

L-Citrulline HCL

L-Citrulline is an amino acid the body can recycle back into arginine, helping to support a more continuous cycle of nitric oxide production. This is included to help support consistent circulation, even during periods of stress, fatigue, or poor sleep.
🍁

L-Citrulline Malate

This form combines citrulline with malate, a compound involved in the body's natural energy production processes at the cellular level. Alongside supporting circulation, it's included to help support the clearing of metabolic byproducts that can contribute to that heavy, fatigued feeling by the end of the day.
🍁

Niacin

Niacin (vitamin B3) is an essential nutrient involved in the production of NAD+, a molecule that plays a central role in how cells generate energy. It's included in VapoCept to help support healthy microcirculation, so the other nutrients in the formula are able to reach where they're needed.
🍁

Beta-Alanine

Beta-Alanine is an amino acid associated with raising levels of carnosine in the body, a natural compound with antioxidant properties. It's included to help support the body's own defenses against everyday oxidative stress, which can accumulate with age and daily demands.
